# How to use the Songa foundation set

This set is the shared memory of Songa: what we are building, how we sound, the facts we stand on, who we serve, who does what, the words we share, and what we have decided. It is a first draft and an invitation. Nothing here is finished, and much of it is meant to be filled in by the people who carry Songa forward.

## Why this set exists

*Status: draft · Source: Joshua, Nov 23*

Songa has grown over ten years through improvisation and many creative voices. That is its gift. It has also left the story spread across more than a dozen websites and many conversations, with goals and history that do not always line up. This set gathers what we know in one place, so the team can build from the same picture, speak to our audience clearly and make content without starting over each time.

It is not a rulebook. Think of it as the notes of a long conversation, written down so the next person can join it.

## How to read a section

*Status: draft*

Every section opens with a short line in italics that says how settled it is and where it came from.

- **Confirmed:** said or decided by Krister or Joshua, with the date. Treat it as true until the Living record says otherwise.
- **Draft:** written from what we have heard and seen, but nobody has signed it off yet. A good working assumption.
- **Needs input:** blank, thin or uncertain. An open question, waiting for the person who knows.

Sources sit in brackets, like [Krister, Nov 19 talk] or [Sponsorship and pricing, Nov 23]. Anything marked [inferred] is our reading of the material, not something anyone said. Lines that start with **Open:** are questions. Lines that start with **Invitation:** are prompts for whoever wants to take a section further; answers come in as change requests.

## For people

*Status: draft*

1. Start with Vision and strategy, then Brand DNA. Together they take ten minutes and give you the feeling of the whole.
2. Use Facts and figures and the Glossary as reference. Check them before anything goes out.
3. If you know the answer to an **Open:** line, send it to Joshua as a change request. Once Krister approves it, Joshua writes it in, marks it confirmed and adds a dated line to the Living record.
4. If something here feels wrong, say so. A correction is the most useful request you can send.

## For any team member's AI

*Status: draft*

These documents are written so an AI assistant can work from them as well as a person can. A few notes for the assistant, offered as guidance rather than commands:

- Read this guide first, then whichever documents the task touches.
- Lean on confirmed lines as facts. Use draft lines as working assumptions and say so when it matters. Treat needs-input sections as unknown: ask, or leave a clear gap, rather than invent.
- Keep [inferred] readings separate from what people said.
- Write in the voice described in Brand DNA, but let it breathe. The voice notes describe a feeling, not a script, and new ideas are welcome.
- When the work produces a new fact or a decision, suggest a line for the Living record so the set keeps growing.

## How the set grows

*Status: confirmed · Source: Joshua, Nov 23, 2026*

Changes flow one way, from the source to the finished pieces, so two copies never disagree.

1. **Request:** anyone on the team asks Joshua for a change.
2. **Approve:** Krister approves it.
3. **Update the source:** Joshua makes the change in the GitHub repository, keeping each section's status and source.
4. **Log it:** Joshua adds a dated line to the Living record: what changed, who asked, that Krister approved, and which Drive files were rebuilt.
5. **Rebuild:** Joshua rebuilds the affected PDFs and puts them in Drive.

When a section fills up, split it rather than let it sprawl. Once a quarter, read the whole set and retire what is no longer true.

**Invitation:** suggest whatever is missing. A section for partners' own stories, a page on the BOULDER.LIVE musical, a map of the websites: if it helps the team, it belongs here.

## Where the set lives

*Status: confirmed · Source: Joshua, Nov 23, 2026*

- **GitHub, the source:** the private repository novasapienlabs/songa-foundation holds the Markdown set. Every change starts here, and any team member's AI reads from here.
- **Google Drive, the finished pieces:** a private folder named Songa holds the PDFs built from the source, for the team to read view-only. The Word files are Joshua's working copies, not a second source.
- **Memory:** the Living record, kept as CHANGELOG.md in the repository, is the one place that remembers what changed and why.
- **Safety net:** a weekly check compares Drive's edit dates with the Living record and flags any edit that isn't logged. Flags go to Joshua first, and only real conflicts go to Krister. It isn't running yet.

**Open:** Who gets access to each? Both are private to Joshua for now.
