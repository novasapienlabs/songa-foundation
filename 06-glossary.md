# 6. Glossary

Songa has its own words. Using them the same way helps the team sound like one voice.

*Status: draft unless marked · Add a word whenever someone has to ask what one means.*

## Places and formats

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Songa | The brand and the experience: the moment a song is born, when someone sings their first song into a mic | confirmed |
| a Songa | A home or space turned into a co-creative space using the Songa format | draft |
| songa.live | The world of Songa and its main web address; always lowercase | confirmed |
| the songa.live house | Songa's home at the Sundance Film Festival, in the Odd Fellows building on Pearl Street | confirmed |
| the back door | The way into the house | draft |
| members-only lounge | The holding room for people past the 415 capacity, open to credential holders | draft |
| the secret lounge | A dusty basement below, perhaps 20 to 30 people, if the fire code allows | needs input |
| Boulder.live | An immersive musical about Boulder culture, and one of Songa's pieces | draft |
| Songa Studios | The name on the pitch and design documents | needs input |

## Things that happen in the house

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| jamversations | Songa's live musical podcast: conversations that turn into improvised songs | confirmed |
| the Songa circle | The music made together in the room | draft |
| the studio | The TV show filmed in real time in the house and on the streets | draft |
| assets within an hour | Partners get the content made with them within an hour | confirmed |
| environment | What Songa creates, as opposed to an event | draft |

## Getting in

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| credential | Songa's word for membership: a time, a place and a reason to meet again. Never a ticket, pass or VIP | confirmed |
| Single Day | A credential for one day | confirmed |
| First Half | A credential for Jan 19 to 25 | confirmed |
| Second Half | A credential for Jan 26 to 31 | confirmed |
| All 12 Days | A credential for the whole festival period | confirmed |
| Event credential | A credential in a different colour, with no company name, for guests of a partner's event | draft |
| Songa account | Where a credential is held, as a QR code | draft |
| partner | A brand, studio or person who supports Songa; "sponsor" also works | confirmed |

## Money and mission

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Patron's Pledge | An idea for investors: invest in the for-profit, pledge the gains to the nonprofit, and get capital back after three years if it isn't sustainable | needs input |
| culture capitalism | Krister's name for the model behind the Patron's Pledge | needs input |
| pay for performance philanthropy | Giving tied to results, part of the same idea | needs input |
| patron investor | Someone who backs Songa as both patron and investor | needs input |
| revillage | To bring back the village: belonging made by creating together | confirmed |
