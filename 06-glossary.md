# 6. Glossary

Songa has its own words. Using them the same way helps the team sound like one voice.

*Status: draft unless marked · Add a word whenever someone has to ask what one means.*

## Places and formats

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Songa | The brand and the experience: the moment a song is born, when someone sings their first song into a mic | confirmed |
| a Songa | A home or space turned into a co-creative space using the Songa format | draft |
| SONGA.LIVE | The world of Songa and its main web address; always in capitals | confirmed [Krister, Nov 23 call] |
| the SONGA.LIVE House | Songa's home at the Sundance Film Festival, in the Odd Fellows building on Pearl Street; House capitalised | confirmed [Krister, Nov 23 call] |
| the bird's nest | The second-floor room by the back elevator, about 50 people; the members' lounge | draft |
| the back door | The members' way in, by the elevator. The public comes in the front door | confirmed |
| members-only lounge | The holding room for people past the 415 capacity, open to credential holders | draft |
| the secret lounge | A dusty basement below, perhaps 20 to 30 people, if the fire code allows | needs input |
| BOULDER.LIVE | A live, immersive musical about Boulder's culture of creativity and belonging, and one of Songa's pieces | draft |
| Songa Studios | The name on the pitch and design documents | needs input |

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| credential | Songa's word for membership: a time, a place and a reason to meet again. A physical badge worn on a lanyard. Never a ticket, pass or VIP | confirmed |
| members | People who hold a credential. Never VIP | confirmed [Krister, Nov 23 call] |
| Single Day | A credential for one day | confirmed |
| First Half | A credential for Jan 19 to 25 | confirmed |
| Second Half | A credential for Jan 26 to 31 | confirmed |
| All 12 Days | A credential for the whole festival period | confirmed |
| Event credential | A credential in a different colour, with no company name, for guests of a partner's event | draft |
| Songa account | Where a credential is held, as a QR code | draft |
| partner | A brand, studio or person who supports Songa; "sponsor" also works | confirmed |
