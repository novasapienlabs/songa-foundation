# 2. Brand DNA

Songa is the feeling of wonder, awe and grace when artistic beauty quiets a room, and a singer — or a song — is born.

## The heart of it

*Status: confirmed · Sources: Sponsorship and pricing, Nov 23; Songa foundation*

- **Essence:** the feeling of wonder, awe and grace when artistic beauty quiets a room, and a singer — or a song — is born.
- **Brand words:** wonder, awe, grace.
- **The name:** Songa is the moment a song is born, when someone sings their first song into a microphone.
- **An echo:** "Songa" sits close to "sanga," a word Boulder's spiritual and creative communities use for a gathering. Krister sees that as a happy resonance [Krister, Nov 23].

**Invitation:** tell the story of the first time someone sang into a Songa mic. A real moment will carry the brand further than any definition.

## How Songa sounds

*Status: draft · Source: [inferred] from the foundation, the pricing doc and Krister's talks*

Songa sounds like a warm host at the door of a house you have never been in. It is calm, curious and generous. It invites more than it explains, and it leaves room for the reader to wonder.

- **Warm, not salesy.** We invite people in; we don't push.
- **Quiet, not loud.** Short sentences, plain words, space between ideas.
- **Peers, not performers and audience.** Patrons and artists make things together.
- **Mysterious, not secretive.** We hint at what is behind the back door, and we are honest about everything that matters.
- **Playful.** Songa can smile at itself.

**Invitation:** collect three pieces of writing that sound exactly like Songa, and three that don't. Examples teach a voice faster than rules.

## How Songa lives in the world

*Status: confirmed · Source: Songa foundation*

- **Off the grid on purpose:** Songa's 30 or more websites are hidden from Google, AI search and social. It grows by word of mouth.
- **The ask to guests:** "Please don't share our art on social media. Sit beside someone you love and share social moments instead."
- **Media goes back to the people in it:** everything recorded is given back to those who made it.
- **We call:** in The WORLD OF SONGA, people dial a phone number. There is one public number and no public email: "Call us, no email." That is only the first contact; emails can follow the call [Krister, Nov 23 call].

**Open:** A question for Krister: which of the 30 or more sites is the front door, and which should be retired? Joshua doesn't know yet [Joshua, Nov 23, 2026].

## The look

*Status: colours confirmed [Joshua, Nov 23, 2026]; the rest draft · Sources: Songa Document Brand Guide v1.0 (proposed); Nov 23 notes*

- **Primary colours:** Campfire Orange #FF6F61, Portal Blue #007AFF, Parchment White #FDFBF7, Charcoal Black #1C1C1C. Emotionally warm and creatively bold [Joshua, Nov 23, 2026].
- **Secondary colours:** Smoke Gray #5B5B5B, Warm Beige #E8DCCF, and Golden Ember #FFB347, used sparingly as an accent [Joshua, Nov 23, 2026]. They replace the guide's earlier palette.
- **Legibility:** small text stays in Charcoal Black or Smoke Gray. Campfire Orange and Golden Ember carry shapes and accents, and Portal Blue carries large type, lines and badges [inferred: contrast check, Nov 23].
- **The sponsor deck:** white pages, blue and a lighter blue instead of purple, with Campfire Orange as the accent [Krister, Nov 23 call]. Krister confirmed Campfire Orange and Portal Blue as the palette "we designed a couple years ago". The lighter blue in the deck is a tint of Portal Blue, #DCEBFF [Joshua's team, Nov 24].
- **Type:** Georgia for titles, Arial for body text.
- **Logo:** the new Flatirons Songa logo (received Nov 23) replaces the older colour logo with the St. Louis skyline.
- **Design system:** "a quiet visual language for the work of bringing people together," with six badges: threshold, refrain, ensemble, listening, cue and curtain.
- **Partner mark:** the Sundance Institute official partner mark is still to come. The file in hand is the festival's own logo [Joshua, email, Nov 23].

**Open:** Is the design system v1.0 adopted, or still a proposal?
