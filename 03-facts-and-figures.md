## What runs in the house

*Status: draft · Sources: Songa foundation; Sponsorship and pricing, Nov 23*

- **Immersive experience:** the house itself, with music in the walls.
- **Musical podcast ("jamversations"):** conversations that turn into improvised songs, on an 8-camera shoot, roughly 10am to 6pm.
- **Music:** the Songa circle and a 6 to 12 piece band.
- **Studio:** a TV show filmed in real time in the spaces and, if the City of Boulder agrees, on Boulder's streets.
- **Boulder.live:** an immersive musical about Boulder culture.
- **Cameras:** 8 to 12 roving cameras, projected into the room.
- **Assets:** partners get their content within an hour.

## Credentials

*Status: confirmed · Source: Sponsorship and pricing, Nov 23. That document is the source of truth.*

Entry to the house is free. A credential adds skip-the-line and backstage access, food and drink, and a chance to take part in the music, the podcast and the TV show. It is bought by phone, held in a Songa account as a QR code, and swapped for a wristband or badge at the door. It can be passed on to someone else.

| Credential | Price | Saving against $500 a day |
|---|---|---|
| Single Day | $500 | |
| First Half (Jan 19 to 25) | $2,000 | $1,500 |
| Second Half (Jan 26 to 31) | $1,500 | $1,500 |
| All 12 Days | $2,500 | $4,000 |
