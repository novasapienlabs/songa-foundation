## What runs in the house

*Status: draft · Sources: Songa foundation; Sponsorship and pricing, Nov 23*

- **Immersive experience:** the house itself, with music echoing through the halls.
- **Musical podcast (Jamversations):** a podcast with live music, where songs are improvised from the conversation in real time, on an 8-camera cinematic shoot, roughly 10am to 6pm. Krister calls it Songa's biggest selling point [Nov 23 call].
- **Music:** the Songa circle and a 6 to 12 piece band.
- **Studio:** a TV show filmed in real time in the spaces and, pending permission from the City of Boulder, on Boulder's streets.
- **BOULDER.LIVE:** a live, immersive musical about Boulder's culture of creativity and belonging.
- **Cameras:** 8 to 12 roving cameras, projected into the room.
- **Assets:** all video and music assets within an hour of stepping off the microphones, ready to clip.
- **Food and drink:** free for credential holders, inside or from food trucks. Whether food can be cooked in the building is still being checked with Odd Fellows (no hood; warming is fine) [Nov 23 call].
- **Tickets:** Sundance Institute doesn't allow public ticket sales. Krister is asking Sundance Institute whether a one-day membership can be sold inside the house [Nov 23 call].

**Open:** How many podcast slots run each day?

## Credentials

*Status: confirmed · Source: Sponsorship and pricing, Nov 23. That document is the source of truth.*

Entry to the house is free. A credential adds skip-the-line and backstage access, food and drink, and a chance to take part in the music, the podcast and the TV show. It is bought by phone, held in a Songa account as a QR code, and swapped at the door for a physical plastic badge on a plain lanyard (the badge carries the Songa name, the lanyard doesn't) [Nov 23 call]. It can be passed on to someone else.

The $500 list price stays here for working out savings, but member-facing pieces no longer show it [Krister, Nov 23 call].

- A private room or conversation space for 4 hours comes with Tiny House and up, subject to availability.
- Turnkey event planning (food, music, 8-camera shoot) comes with Small House and up.

- At $50K and $100K, the partner's brand is woven into BOULDER.LIVE, with a custom song for the film or brand.

- The Small House column ($20K) is the one the deck highlights.
- Podcast slot: 30 minutes at $20K, 45 minutes at $30K, 1 hour at $50K and up. The deck's table shows prices as $2.5K to $100K+ [Krister, via Joshua, Nov 24].
- Every package can be tailored. Renting the house outright is rare.
