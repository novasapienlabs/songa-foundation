# Change requests

Anyone on the Songa team can ask for a change to this set. Joshua gathers the requests, and Krister approves them. [Joshua, Nov 23, 2026]

## How a change happens

1. **Ask:** send Joshua what you'd change, where (file and section), and why. Say where it comes from: something Krister said, a document, a decision, or your own suggestion.
2. **Review:** Joshua collects requests and brings them to Krister.
3. **Decide:** Krister approves, adjusts or declines the change.
4. **Update:** Joshua makes the approved change in this repository and in the Google Drive copies, so the two stay in step.
5. **Record:** Joshua adds a dated line to `07-living-record.md`: date, what changed, who decided, where it came from.

## A good request

- Names the file and section.
- Gives the new wording, or the gap to fill.
- Says where it came from, so the section's status and source can be set right.
- Leaves a blank as a blank when nobody knows yet. An honest gap is better than a guess.

## Open

- Who gets access to this repository and to the Drive folder.
- Whether team members send requests by email, message, or a GitHub issue.
