# 8. The Songa story

Songa is the feeling of wonder, awe and grace when artistic beauty quiets a room, and a singer — or a song — is born. This document tries to make you feel it before the other seven explain it.

*Status: draft · Written from the other seven documents and Krister's own words where quoted. Not yet reviewed by Krister. Every line marked **Krister to tell** is a blank only he can fill.*

## How to use this document

*Status: draft*

- **Read it first.** The other documents hold the facts. This one holds the feeling they serve. When you write, design or speak for Songa, start here and check the facts there.
- **The manifesto** can be quoted in team material once Krister has approved it.
- **The night** is imagined, not a record. It is built only from what is planned for the SONGA.LIVE House, so every detail is real, but the people and moments in it are made up. Replace them with true stories as they happen.
- **The blanks are the point.** Songa's real story lives with Krister and the people who have been in the room. The questions at the end are written so he can answer them by voice.
- **Tell it without founders.** Songa names no founders and is close to an open-source brand. The story belongs to the room and the people in it, not to one person [Brand DNA].

## The manifesto

*Status: draft · Sources: Brand DNA; Vision and strategy; Krister's own lines where quoted. For Krister to reshape.*

Somewhere in every room there is a song that hasn't been sung yet.

We make the space for it.

Not a stage with an audience in front of it. A house, with people in it. Patrons and artists, strangers and old friends, side by side as peers. Someone starts talking. Someone starts playing. And the conversation turns, without anyone quite deciding, into music.

Then it happens. Artistic beauty quiets the room, and a singer — or a song — is born. That feeling has three names: wonder, awe and grace.

We believe there is a Creator within everyone. Most of us were simply never handed the microphone.

We believe in the village. Not as a memory, but as something we can build again, one house and one song at a time. We are a scalable community franchise to revillage the world.

We believe art should pay the people who make it. What partners give goes to the artists performing in the space.

We believe what we make together belongs to the people who made it. Everything recorded here goes back to the people in it.

We believe in being in the room. We're not on social media, but we are very social. So we ask one thing: sit beside someone you love and share the moment instead.

We don't make events. We make environments.

Songa travels the oldest way there is, from one person to another. If you are reading this, someone thought you should.

Welcome to The WORLD OF SONGA, where we awaken
the Creator within.

## A night at the SONGA.LIVE House

*Status: draft, imagined · Built from Facts and figures, Glossary and Krister's descriptions of the house. The people and moments are made up; the places and plans are real. Replace with true stories as they happen.*

**Before.** It starts with a person, not an ad. A friend, or someone you met at a screening that afternoon, says: meet me at six. There is no website to check and no feed to scroll. There is a phone number, and there is their word.

**Pearl Street.** January in Boulder, while the 2027 Sundance Film Festival is in town. Cold air, the Flatirons gone dark behind the town. The front door of the old Odd Fellows building is open to anyone, free. You walk round to the back. Your friend is waiting by the door with a second credential and hands it to you. A code on a phone becomes a badge on a plain lanyard. On the pavement, someone with a notebook, who says they're a journalist, asks what brought you to Boulder. One of the characters, perhaps. You can't quite tell.

**The nest.** The back elevator opens on the second floor, into the members-only lounge everyone calls the bird's nest. Couches, corners made for conversation, something warm to eat and something to drink. Music is echoing through the halls from somewhere below. Nobody asks what you do. Somebody asks what you love.

**The studio.** Down the hall, two people are talking in front of the cameras while a band listens. Then the band answers. A phrase from the conversation comes back as a melody, then a chorus. That is a Jamversation. Cameras drift through the house and out onto the street, and what they catch appears on the walls around you, so the show and the room become the same thing. A host catches your eye. Would you like to sit in?

**The moment.** *Imagined. Krister to tell a real one.* Later the room fills. The band is a dozen players now, and the audience is slowly becoming a band too: someone finds a rhythm on the table, someone is humming the harmony. Then someone steps up who has never sung into a microphone. The talking stops. Glasses are set down. For a few minutes, artistic beauty quiets the room, and a song is born. Nobody holds up a phone. Everybody will remember it.

**Before you go.** Within the hour, the video and the music from your conversation are yours to keep. You meet your friend at the back door again to hand the credential back, and you make a plan to meet tomorrow. That was the point all along: a time, a place and a reason to meet again.

**On the way home.** Pearl Street is quiet. You are humming something that didn't exist two hours ago. And a thought arrives: we could do this at our house. That is how a Songa begins.

## Where Songa comes from

*Status: needs input · Sources: Vision and strategy; Brand DNA; Facts and figures*

What we know:

- The work began about ten years ago and was built quietly. Krister's phrase is "an overnight success, ten years in the making."
- Every piece already exists and grows a little each day: the platform, the writers' room, the characters, BOULDER.LIVE, a short film, a book and a building.
- The book, the experience and the pitch are different doors into the same story.
- Songa grew through improvisation and many creative voices, across 30 or more websites.
- The nonprofit exists today in Missouri, Songa's earlier logo carried the St. Louis skyline, and its phone number has a St. Louis area code. So Songa's roots may be in St. Louis before Boulder [inferred].
- "Songa" sits close to "sanga", a word Boulder's spiritual and creative communities use for a gathering [Krister].

## Questions for Krister

*Status: needs input · For a voice memo. Say the number, then talk. Stories are better than definitions, and rough is fine.*

1. **The first night:** take us back to the first Songa. Where were you, who was in the room, and what happened?
2. **The moment:** tell us about one time a room went quiet and a song was born. What did it sound like, and what did people do right after?
3. **Ten years:** why did you keep going? What would have been lost if you had stopped?
4. **A creator awakened:** describe one person who walked in as audience and left as a creator. No names needed.
5. **On the way out:** what have people said to you after a Songa night? Any lines you still remember word for word?
6. **The characters:** who are they, and what does each one give a community?
7. **The first and last minute:** when someone walks through the back door of the SONGA.LIVE House in January, what should they feel in their first minute, and in their last?
8. **Revillage:** what does the word mean to you? Where did you last feel the village?
9. **Success:** if Songa goes further than you hope, what does the world look like in ten years? One sentence is plenty.
10. **The mystery:** is there anything about Songa people should only discover by being there, that we should never write down?

When the answers come in, they replace the blanks above, the imagined moment in the night becomes a true one, and each change gets a line in the Living record.
