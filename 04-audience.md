# 4. Audience

Songa speaks to people who want beauty and belonging in their lives: patrons who give, partners who host, guests who come in, and the artists at the center of it.

## What everyone has in common

*Status: draft · Sources: Songa foundation; Krister, Nov 19 and Nov 23*

- **Before they arrive:** at home, curious, a little intrigued. Usually someone they trust told them about Songa.
- **When they leave:** a sense of wonder, and a reason to meet again.
- **What they share:** they would rather be in the room than watch it on a screen.

**Invitation:** write down three real people who have come through the door, what brought them, and what they said on the way out.

## What moves each group

*Status: draft · Source: [inferred] from the talks and documents*

- **Patrons:** a personal invitation, the story of the artists their gift supports, and the feeling of being a peer, not a donor.
- **Partners:** being seen at Sundance in a way nobody else offers, and content they own within the hour.
- **Credential holders:** a time, a place and a reason to meet again.
- **Artists:** fair pay and being treated as co-creators.

**Invitation:** test these with real conversations and replace the guesses with what people actually say.
