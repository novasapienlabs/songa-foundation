## How decisions get made

*Status: draft · Sign-off confirmed by Joshua, Nov 23, 2026*

Nothing is written down yet about who decides what. A light starting point, offered as a suggestion:

1. Krister decides on vision, brand and partnerships.
2. The person who owns a role decides inside it, and tells the team.
3. Anything with money, law or the public goes to Krister before it goes out.
4. Every decision gets a dated line in the Living record.

**Confirmed:** Krister signs off on documents and prices before they reach a partner [Joshua, Nov 23, 2026].

**Confirmed:** anyone on the team can request a change to this set. Requests go to Joshua, and Krister approves them before they go in [Joshua, Nov 23, 2026].

## Four kinds of energy

*Status: draft · Source: adapted from the BizBrain roles idea*

A creative venture needs four kinds of energy. Most people carry one or two. Naming them helps a team see what it has and what it lacks.

- **Dreamer:** holds the vision and the feeling. Songa has a lot of this.
- **Thinker:** turns the vision into a plan, a model and numbers.
- **Storyteller:** carries the story to the people who need to hear it.
- **Leader:** gets people moving together and keeps promises.

**Invitation:** put a name next to each, and notice where the gaps are.
